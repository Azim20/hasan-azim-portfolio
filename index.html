About Me

From the factory floor to working systems

Textile Engineer by training. Industrial Engineer by practice. Now in Sourcing — and a builder throughout.

Today I'm a Merchandiser in Sourcing & Supply Chain at Jinnat Fashion Ltd., DBL Group, working on the accessories and trims side: raising purchase requisitions in Oracle EBS R12, costing and booking against the Master BOM, holding budgets, and chasing suppliers so the line never waits on a trim. It is exactly the kind of work I like — high volume, unforgiving detail, and full of things a computer should be doing instead of a person.

That project is below, and it is the clearest example of what I actually do: know the process well enough to automate it, then build the thing.

Everything here I build myself with AI as my co-builder (I use Claude), pairing domain knowledge with AI to ship fast — and I'm ready to lead AI and automation inside apparel operations, exactly where this industry is heading. Flagship · ERP Automation● IN DAILY USE · v2.0.1

Oracle PR Booking Bot

Purchase requisition automation for Oracle EBS R12 · DBL Group

Our sourcing team was raising 30–50 purchase requisitions a day by hand — the same long click-path through Oracle, every time, with a mistake costing real money. I built a bot that drives Oracle itself: Python + Playwright behind a local FastAPI dashboard, so a merchandiser drops in a file, watches live progress, and gets a report with the PR numbers at the end.

  • 1Reads the Master BOM, checks the remaining budget, and uploads the items that aren't on it yet as a generated CSV.
  • 2For items already on the BOM it replaces wastage, booking %, quantity, price and supplier — matching columns by caption, never by position, and comparing numbers as numbers.
  • 3Raises the requisition — but only after proving the ticked rows are exactly ours. A PR number is the only accepted proof of success.

Built to be safe before it was built to be fast: a dry run stages everything and presses Cancel, a negative budget stops the run before any edit, and nothing reports success it cannot prove. Every live failure became a regression test — the suite is now 1,149 checks.
