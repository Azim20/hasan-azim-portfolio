About Me

From the factory floor to working systems

Textile Engineer by training. Industrial Engineer by practice. A builder by instinct.

I started on the floor as a hands-on Line IE — making sewing line layouts, running the lines, and managing large operator teams with full floor responsibility. From there I grew into IE Executive and now Central IE. Today I'm in the Project Opex & CI team (Operational Excellence & Continuous Improvement) at Snowtex Group — a central role across the whole group. As Central IE I monitor, support and train the IE teams of both factories, SOL (Snowtex Outerwear) and SSL (Snowtex Sportswear). As MTO Leader I guide each new Management Trainee Officer batch through their full six-month program — their classes, problem know-how, and everything about how Snowtex runs.

I'm a core member of Snowtex's factory automation & IoT initiative: operator-level production-counting devices, auto-generated capacity, QR-code material tracking from cutting to store, and an individual operator KPI system with an audit panel. Alongside that, I build my own tools — Android apps, Python automation, and HTML web apps — each one born from a real problem I hit at work. I build them myself with AI as my co-builder (I use Claude), pairing my domain knowledge with AI to ship fast — and I'm ready to lead AI integration inside factory operations, exactly where the industry is heading.

I also handle the international buyer Decathlon: I follow the INDUS line, own OWE and QCO for Decathlon lines, and lead the weekly Monday SUM meeting — presenting root-cause analysis and agreeing action plans. Outside work, I run the "Study Material BD" YouTube channel and have self-published a children's book on Amazon KDP.
